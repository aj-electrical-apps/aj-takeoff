Zoom

Scroll the mouse wheel to zoom in and out. To move around, hold the right mouse button and drag (Space or the wheel button work too), or turn on the arrows button. On a tablet, pinch with two fingers. Click a symbol to highlight it. Drag across the sheet to highlight everything in an area, then delete it all at once. Right-click a symbol for more.
